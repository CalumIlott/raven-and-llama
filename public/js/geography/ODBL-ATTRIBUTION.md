# Country fact data attribution

The country facts in `country-facts-data.js` are derived from the [countries dataset by mledoze](https://github.com/mledoze/countries), including its official language, currency, area, and border fields. The source dataset is available under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. This derived country fact database is offered under the same license; see [ODBL-LICENSE.txt](ODBL-LICENSE.txt) and the [upstream project](https://github.com/mledoze/countries).

The pre-existing country highlights remain attributed individually to their listed sources in the app.
